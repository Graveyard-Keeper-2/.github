## What Is Graveyard Keeper 2?

Graveyard Keeper 2 is a dark medieval management simulation developed by Lazy Bear Games and published by tinyBuild. It continues the unusual world of the original game, combining cemetery management, town restoration, crafting, resource gathering, automation, and battles against the undead.

Players take on the role of the Grand Inquisitor and face a town threatened by a zombie outbreak. Alongside maintaining the cemetery, they must help restore buildings, manage local residents, gather materials, and prepare forces to defend the settlement.

A central mechanic is the use of zombies as workers. These undead helpers can assist with repetitive tasks, allowing players to create connected crafting systems and manage resources across multiple workstations.

The game also expands the series with tactical combat. Players can assemble an undead army, equip units, build defensive structures, and fight enemy forces to reclaim areas of the town.

## Core Gameplay Features

### Cemetery Management

The cemetery remains at the heart of the game. Players manage burial grounds, maintain the surrounding area, and develop facilities that support their wider activities.

Cemetery management connects with the game's resource systems, giving players reasons to gather materials, construct new facilities, and keep the grounds organized.

### Town Restoration

The town is a major part of the sequel. Players work to restore buildings, help residents, and reclaim districts affected by the undead threat.

Restoration tasks connect exploration, resource gathering, crafting, and combat. Rebuilding the settlement also opens additional activities and services as the adventure advances.

### Zombie Workforce

Zombies can be assigned to suitable tasks around the settlement. Their work helps reduce repetitive manual activity and supports the game's automation systems.

Players can organize workers around different stations and resource needs. Managing their assignments becomes an important part of keeping crafting chains running smoothly.

### Crafting and Automation

The game features interconnected crafting stations, machinery, and resource-handling systems. Players gather raw materials, process them into useful goods, and organize work areas to support larger tasks.

Automation becomes increasingly useful as the settlement grows. A well-arranged network of stations can reduce the amount of manual work required to maintain supplies.

## Undead Army and Tactical Combat

### Recruiting Undead Forces

The undead army is a major addition to the series. Players assemble forces to fight hostile groups and help reclaim the town.

Unit selection and army preparation matter when facing different enemy formations. The right combination of troops can make a battle easier to manage.

### Defensive Structures

Players can strengthen their position with defensive structures such as barricades and other battlefield obstacles. These defenses help control enemy movement and support the army during combat.

### Combat Equipment and Potions

Equipment and consumables can help prepare for battles. Players can gather materials, craft useful items, and choose suitable supplies before engaging hostile forces.

### Reclaiming the Town

Combat is connected to the larger restoration objective. Winning battles helps clear areas and supports the effort to bring the town back under control.

## Resource Gathering and Crafting Systems

### Collecting Materials

The world contains plants, minerals, crafting ingredients, and other useful resources. Gathering materials supports cemetery management, building, crafting, and town restoration.

### Machinery and Workstations

Players can create and use machinery to process resources. Different stations serve different functions, so arranging them effectively helps maintain a reliable flow of materials.

### Farming and Gardening

Cultivating plants and collecting natural resources support crafting and other settlement activities. Farming adds another layer to resource planning as the number of available tasks increases.

### Inventory and Resource Planning

Storage and material organization become important when many crafting stations are active. Keeping useful supplies available helps avoid interruptions and makes larger tasks easier to manage.

## Town Residents and Dark Humor

Graveyard Keeper 2 continues the series' distinctive combination of medieval fantasy, absurd situations, and dark comedy.

Residents have their own requests and needs, while town restoration creates new interactions and tasks. Players must balance local demands with cemetery duties, crafting activities, and the defense of the settlement.

The writing and character interactions use exaggerated humour rather than a strictly realistic historical tone.
